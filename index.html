How to play

A game of nerve & six dice

One more roll?

Keep what counts. Bank what you can. Know when to stop.

Every roll is a choice.

Roll six dice and keep at least one scoring die or combination. Bank your turn points, or roll the remaining dice. A roll with no scoring dice is a Farkle: you lose the points from that turn.

Combinations must come from a single roll. Score all six dice and you have hot dice: you may roll all six again, keeping your turn points.

Choosing Bank or Roll commits your selection from the current roll. Four ones score 1,000 as a group; they cannot be kept as a triplet plus a single for 1,100. Three pairs means three different faces.

Bank at least 500 points in one turn to get on the board. After that, you can bank any scoring turn. Once someone banks 10,000, every other player gets exactly one final turn. The highest score wins; tied leaders play equal-turn sudden-death rounds.

Your first turn
  1. Start a local table, or create an online room and invite friends. Online players choose Ready to play before the host starts.
  2. Roll one die each. The highest starts; tied leaders roll again.
  3. On your turn, roll six dice. Select single 1s or 5s, or a whole scoring combination. Then choose Bank to end your turn, or Roll to throw the dice you did not keep.
  4. Every Bank or Roll keeps your selection first. The Roll button shows the chance that the next throw has no scoring dice.
  5. A Farkle loses your unbanked points. Choose Pass the dice to hand over the turn.

Use Tab to move between controls and Space or Enter to activate them. Each die on the table is a button that announces its value and selection; Scoring hints marks dice that can score. Reduced motion follows your device settings; sound is optional.

Online rooms and reconnecting

Rooms hold two to eight guests. Share the invite link before starting; late visitors can spectate. Reload in the same browser to reclaim your seat. Opening the same seat in another tab transfers control to that tab.

The host can enable a 60-second decision clock. A disconnected player has 30 seconds to return. After a timeout, points already kept are banked if legal; otherwise the turn is forfeited. Dice selected but not yet banked or rolled do not count. The host role passes to a connected player when needed.

Guest names, scores and dice are visible to everyone in the room. Your reconnect token stays in this browser. Unused rooms expire after 24 hours. Local matches save in this browser; clearing browser data removes that save and any guest seat tokens.
